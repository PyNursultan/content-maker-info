How Content Maker uses Google data

This policy describes the owner-operated Content Maker deployment and the Google connections enabled by its operator.

Data accessed and purposes

When authorized, Content Maker uses Google account authorization tokens to perform the selected workflow. Its YouTube integration can access channel identifiers and video or publication metadata, upload video files, and set publication schedules. Source-media connections can access files the operator authorizes for processing.

The separate Google Drive backup connection uses https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It stores encrypted recovery archives, lists application-accessible backup files, and downloads backups to check their integrity or perform operator-requested recovery. A recovery archive may contain application database records, videos and other media, operational configuration, provider receipts, and encrypted credential material.

Storage and protection

Operational records and media are stored on the operator's server. OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted service credentials or restricted service files. Backups are encrypted with age before being uploaded to Google Drive. The recovery identity is retained separately from the cloud archive. Access to the operational workspace, account credentials, and recovery keys is restricted to the operator and authorized service processes.

Sharing and external providers

Google processes the API requests needed for the authorized Google connections. GitHub Pages hosts this public information site. Other media or AI providers may be used for video workflows enabled by the operator; the backup workflow does not send its Google OAuth credentials or unencrypted recovery archive to those providers.

Content Maker does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google data is used to provide the operator-requested application features.

Retention, removal, and revocation

Database records, operational receipts, media, and backups are retained for the operator's workflow and recovery needs. This repair does not introduce a fixed automatic cloud-deletion schedule. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically remove files already stored in Drive or on the server.

The account owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account connections and remove backup files from their own Drive. Contact the operator before removing active local records, recovery keys, or the last verified recovery copy. Revocation may affect other connections sharing the same Google application grant.

This public site

These static pages contain no application login, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or forms that collect account credentials. GitHub may process ordinary hosting request data under its privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For access, removal, or privacy questions, contact the operator through the support email shown on the Content Maker Google consent screen or the project support page. Do not post credentials or private records in public issues. This page will be updated when the described data practices change.

Application information

Content Maker

Content Maker is an owner-operated workspace for preparing videos, managing publication schedules, and keeping encrypted recovery backups.

Google connections

The operator connects Google accounts through Google's authorization screen. Each connection is used for the requested workflow:

  • YouTube: identify the connected channel, read video and publication metadata, and upload or schedule videos when the operator enables publishing.
  • Google Drive backups: upload encrypted Content Maker backups, download them for integrity checks, and support operator-controlled recovery. This connection requests the drive.file permission for files used by the application.
  • Media import: process authorized source files when the operator enables a separate import connection.

Backup authorization is separate from YouTube authorization. Backups are encrypted before they are uploaded to Google Drive.

This public site describes the application and its data practices. The operational workspace and backup archives require the operator's authorization.

Privacy and support

Read the Content Maker privacy policy for details about Google data, storage, and access controls. For support, use the support email displayed on the application's Google consent screen or the project support page. Do not include tokens, keys, or private backup contents in a public issue.
